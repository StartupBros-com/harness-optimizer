# Bounded Checkpoints

Use the bundled [reviewer](../../../agents/harness-optimizer-reviewer.md) in fresh,
read-only contexts with the host's inherited model. Each stage's reviewers receive
the same exact claim/proposal plus named relevant files or a bounded evidence
packet, their assigned lenses and the observed revision/digest. Freeze the shared
subject fields once before either lens is dispatched, then copy them unchanged;
vary only lens-specific instructions outside the shared evidence. Include authority
and atomicity facts in both application lenses, not only authority-recovery.
The claim is an object of review, not proof. At diagnosis send the provisional selection plus
deduplicated evidence, coverage and remainder, including the scoped causal diagnosis,
source-event mapping and importance/readiness rationale, not every raw record.
Include the concrete patch at application, and the
exact proposed final report at outcome. Outcome evidence includes actual attempts
and check results at the final revision. Accept and emit only that reviewed report
at that revision; a material report or source change needs a changed-evidence
follow-up within the existing budget. Changed target content invalidates diagnosis
and application evidence tied to the old content: refresh affected checkpoints
with the original lenses within the existing follow-up budget, or leave the write
incomplete. Packets exclude secrets, unrelated history
and mutation authority.
Fully assemble the complete assignment before Agent dispatch; do not launch
unassigned, placeholder or probe agents of any role. Failed or refused reviewer
launches remain observable attempts and consume the existing review budget.
Use the host's supported agent mechanism; if none exists, required independent
review is unavailable, not a solo review advertised as independent.

## Dispatch budget

- After discovery's collection barrier and central reconciliation, provisionally
  select at most three deduplicated causal families (or the operator's smaller limit)
  before diagnosis dispatch. Acceptance follows diagnosis reconciliation; discovery
  attempts remain separate from this review budget.
- Review the batch, never per-lane reviews or one agent per finding. Each applicable stage has two
  reviewers; a third requires a named trust-boundary or cross-runtime concern.
- Diagnosis is applicable to inspected evidence, including a conclusion that
  none supports a current problem or useful change. Review that no-change
  diagnosis even when no family is selected for repair; no evidence available
  to inspect makes diagnosis inapplicable. Proposed application covers prepared
  changes, outcome covers actual applied work, including finishing missing checks
  of prior applied work on interrupted continuation even when this run makes no
  edit. Empty proposals skip application; no applied work skips outcome. Say which
  stages were inapplicable; inapplicable is not passed. An unresolved material diagnosis objection prevents
  acceptance of the affected no-change conclusion, not independent work.
- Allow one follow-up per stage when named new observations, changed hunks or a
  genuinely narrowed claim address the objection. Send the exact changed packet
  to the original lenses, at most that stage's original reviewer count. The
  reviewer must verify whether the objection is resolved; changing prose alone
  never dismisses it. Rephrasing, citation reordering, a timeout or an unchanged
  packet is not changed evidence. No unchanged retry.
- Three stages × three reviewers × two rounds = 18 calls maximum for the run;
  normally six calls. Count attempts, including failed/time-out calls. Keep the
  count in the current result; no persistent scheduler or budget service.

## Assigned lenses

| Stage                | Reviewer 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Reviewer 2                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Diagnosis            | `causal-validity`: refute the alleged root cause; identify duplicated symptoms and plausible alternative causes                                                                                                                              | `evidence-coverage`: refute exhaustive claims, correction/friction equivalence and investigation-contaminated counts                                        |
| Proposed application | `remedy-regression`: refute the patch's behavioral benefit and retained behavior                                                                                                                                                             | `authority-recovery`: inspect the concrete operation, effective installation, invocation, consent, atomicity and recovery                                   |
| Outcome              | `attribution-regression`: inspect actual edits and the latest applicable checks at the cited revision, not narrated intent; refute benefit credited to lost coverage and require comparable measurements or a demonstrated comparable subset | `completeness-activation`: account for every selected family and known pending/declined decision; distinguish source, checks, activation and field evidence |

Both mandatory roles remain required when an optional third reviewer is clean.
The synthetic adapter owns fixed instructions for these IDs, rejects aliases or
irrelevant lenses, and names an optional lens as `trust-boundary:<named concern>`
or `cross-runtime:<named concern>`. These fixture IDs do not add a production
authorization mechanism. Fixture reviewers receive the same adapter protocol and
closed report vocabulary as the coordinator.

The optional third reviewer addresses only its named trust-boundary or
cross-runtime concern. Reviewers return objections with evidence locations,
consequences and a falsifiable check, or no supported objection. They do not
vote, authorize, implement, invent controls or run a second optimization pass.

## Reconciliation

Deduplicate objections by cause and target before any next-stage dispatch.
Resolve them against actual evidence. A material supported objection blocks
only the dependent operation or acceptance. Continue independent work. When a
required reviewer is unavailable, report missing independent evidence; never
replace it with agreement from remaining reviewers. Preserve the host's normal
security/release review requirements; these checkpoints do not replace them.

Use the latest applicable check at the current revision, not an earlier success.
A later failed check invalidates prior acceptance, including outcome reviews
already completed. A new passing check requires outcome reviews after it with
the original lenses and changed evidence, within the same follow-up budget.

## Asynchronous delivery and exact reports

Code and completed checks bind exact content revisions. Release review, CI, owner
handoff and activation are separate observations: record each source, time and
associated revision, with honest pending or unknown status. Include these observations
in the exact outcome-review report. A harmless timestamped draft/ready or queue
observation may remain historical after progress; do not rewrite it merely to be
current or silently upgrade it after review. A handoff ends responsibility only where
host rules allow and does not prove merge, activation or benefit. Acceptance of a
limited report does not satisfy pending required checks or release gates.

Before emission reconcile the latest target and applicable checks. A later failed
check, changed target, new concrete pending decision or materially changed report
cannot hide behind historical snapshots: refresh affected evidence with the original
lenses and the existing changed-evidence follow-up, or report acceptance incomplete.
A later passing check or stronger activation claim requires applicable outcome review
before it is claimed. Preserve the single follow-up and attempt ceiling; add no
checkpoint, budget, monitor or ownership system to track asynchronous progress.
